Testing Framework

Don't just ask “Which ad got more leads?”

Creative performance should be evaluated across the funnel. A low-cost lead is not necessarily a valuable lead.

01Hook / Thumb-stopDoes the creative earn attention?
02CTRDoes attention turn into intent?
03CPLHow efficiently are leads generated?
04QualificationAre the leads relevant?
05Lead-to-QuoteDo qualified leads become opportunities?
Example Optimization Decision

How I would interpret the results

SCENARIO

Creative B produces the lowest CPL, but Creative C generates fewer leads with a stronger lead-to-quote rate.

DECISION

Do not automatically scale Creative B. Compare downstream lead quality and quote outcomes before reallocating budget.

NEXT TEST

Combine Creative C's testimonial angle with Creative B's low-friction offer to test a new hybrid variation.
